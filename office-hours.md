# Office Hours

ESDS no longer offers office hours geared towards Python and data analysis issues.
The High Performance Computing Consulting Services Group (CSG) does still offer office hours,
and they can help with these types of questions.
Please also provide how you'd like to meet (in-person or virtual) and a brief description of your question.

If your question is about an error message you are raising in your workflow,
help them help you by being ready to provide a copy of your dataset and code so we can reproduce your error.
Depending on your question, they may be able to walk you through a solution or send you documentation/resources that address your question.

**NOTE:** Office hours are designed for NSF NCAR and UCAR staff.
